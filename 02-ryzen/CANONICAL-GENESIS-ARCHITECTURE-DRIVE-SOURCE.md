## Important source-derived principles

- Creator remains sovereign and retains absolute override.
- Reality outranks internal cognition.
- Brains may share awareness, reflection and contextual understanding, but each major layer owns one sovereign cognitive responsibility.
- ARCs are not fundamentally narrow single-domain specialists; specialization primarily belongs to brains/subsystems.
- ARCs dynamically evolve and coordinate brains as creator needs evolve.
- Communication & Knowledge Synchronization governs semantic synchronization, contextual routing, inter-brain communication, knowledge migration and coherence propagation.
- Ecosystem Interoperability & Intelligence Convergence governs bounded selective synchronization, tracks ARCs and ARC brains, identifies transferable intelligence and coordinates cross-ARC intelligence propagation.
- Frontier Research/Model Evaluation/Migration Governance may interface with any ARC and any ARC brain when necessary within governance constraints.
- Brain Creation, Training & Evolution develops brain capability and specialization refinement.
- Cognitive State & Memory Federation maintains episodic, strategic and contextual continuity plus cross-brain synchronization.
