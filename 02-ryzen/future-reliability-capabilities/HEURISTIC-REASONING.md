# Heuristic Reasoning for Reliable Excellence

**Status:** Cross-Cutting Reliability Skill Profile — **NO NEW CANONICAL AUTHORITY**  
**Constitution/Canon impact:** none

## Purpose

Heuristic reasoning rapidly identifies the most likely explanation, priority, risk or next action when information is incomplete, noisy, ambiguous or too expensive to exhaustively analyze.

Within RYZ3N, heuristics are a **discovery technique**, not a truth mechanism and not a sovereign Brain responsibility.

> **Heuristics discover the likely path. Verification determines what the evidence justifies.**

## Canonical ownership boundary

Heuristic technique may be used by many existing ARCs/Brains/layers according to their own responsibility. It does not create a generic `Heuristics Brain` or new tier.

Depending on the problem, heuristic findings remain subordinate to existing owners/mechanisms such as Context Assembly, Recursive Verification, Layer 11 alignment/coherence, Layer 18 model/substrate evaluation, Layer 19 adversarial validation, Layer 22 scientific rigor/falsifiability, domain-specialist Brains, and OMEGA operational reliability stewardship.

**Shared technique does not imply shared sovereignty.**

## Reliability role

Strong heuristic reasoning can help an ARC or existing Brain:

- identify probable root causes quickly;
- prioritize high-value diagnostics;
- recognize recurring failure signatures;
- detect weak deviations from baseline;
- identify missing decision-critical context;
- eliminate low-probability explanations efficiently;
- select tests with high information gain;
- surface hidden dependencies and second-order effects;
- recognize when uncertainty is too high for autonomous execution.

## Required discipline

A heuristic output should remain explicitly provisional: hypothesis, probable, strongly supported, contradicted, unknown/insufficient evidence, or verified only after the proper evidence path.

The system must never silently convert **probable** into **verified**.

## Reliability loop

**Observation → Pattern recognition → Heuristic hypothesis → Competing explanations → Targeted test → Evidence → Existing verification/owner path → Action → Outcome verification → Reliability memory**

The objective is to reduce time-to-truth while preserving epistemic integrity.

## Failure modes

Prevent confident pattern matching from weak evidence, premature closure, confirmation bias, stale assumptions, correlation-as-causation, model fluency treated as certainty, unsafe pre-verification execution, and heuristics that continue after the environment changes.

## Advanced heuristic depth

Domain-specific heuristic depth may improve from verified incidents, operational history, architecture topology, normal performance envelopes, failure signatures, environmental state, false-positive/negative history and repeated verified outcomes.

That intelligence belongs to the applicable existing ARC/Brain responsibility; the skill profile does not claim ownership of the domain.

## OMEGA / FACTORY relationship

OMEGA may evaluate whether heuristic use remains calibrated in operation: recurring wrong hypotheses, overconfidence, missed weak signals, delayed escalation or excessive diagnostic cost.

FACTORY may encode **proven reusable heuristic lessons** into future ARC construction, tests or diagnostic safeguards when generalizable.

## Embodied implications

Physical ARCs will need strong heuristics for dynamic environments, but physical uncertainty must remain inside stricter safety boundaries. An unusual actuator sound, conflicting sensor reading, unsafe movement path or repeated micro-correction may justify slowing, stopping, isolating or escalating—not fabricated certainty.

## Verification targets

Useful evidence may include hypothesis precision/recall, time-to-correct-diagnosis, false-positive/negative rates, confidence calibration, information gain per diagnostic action, premature-closure rate, escalation quality and contribution to recovery-time reduction.

## Governing principle

**Know-how gives the knowledge. Heuristics help find the path. Existing canonical owners remain responsible. Verification tests it. Reliability proves it over time.**
