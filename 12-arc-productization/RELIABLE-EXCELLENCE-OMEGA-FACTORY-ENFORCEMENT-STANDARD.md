## Purpose

This standard operationalizes the Founder decision that **Reliable Excellence is RYZ3N Mission Target #1** and that OMEGA and FACTORY carry the strongest day-to-day enforcement responsibility in the current PRIME-era architecture.

The governing red thread is:

**Know-how → High-Quality Execution → Verification → Proven Reliability → Reliable Excellence**

The current maturation line is:

**Engineering Maturity → Proven Reliability → Reliable Excellence**

## Role weighting

### OMEGA — primary quality controller / reliability sentinel

OMEGA continuously evaluates whether ARC state claims are supported by current real-world evidence. OMEGA must challenge unsupported `GREEN`, `PROVEN`, `LIVE`, maturity or excellence claims and escalate material contradictions to PRIME.

OMEGA's standing review surface includes, where applicable:

- correctness;
- responsiveness and latency;
- consistency and predictability;
- health and availability;
- graceful failure;
- recovery and rollback;
- source-driven reconstruction and continuity;
- test and validation evidence;
- observability and diagnostics;
- security, privacy and integrity;
- evidence freshness;
- incident recurrence;
- maturity/status truthfulness.

OMEGA must distinguish one-off success from repeatable dependability.

### FACTORY — primary quality-by-construction manufacturer

FACTORY manufactures the structural conditions that make Reliable Excellence achievable and repeatable.

Each Factory iteration must seek to improve not only ARC creation speed but the repeatability of quality. ARC #N should become:

- faster to manufacture;
- at least as safe;
- at least as reliable;
- easier to validate;
- easier to observe;
- easier to recover;
- less prone to recurring defects;
- less dependent on manual Founder discovery than ARC #N-1.

Factory production should progressively encode:

- deterministic generation;
- schema-validated manifests;
- source-boundary uniqueness;
- isolation/privacy defaults;
- complete runtime-source manifests;
- health and performance hooks;
- automated testing;
- recovery and rollback paths;
- drift detection;
- security/integrity checks;
- evidence production;
- truthful lifecycle/maturity output.

## OMEGA × FACTORY closed quality loop

The standing improvement loop is:

`FACTORY builds quality in → OMEGA verifies quality in reality → OMEGA returns evidence/drift → FACTORY converts reusable lessons into prevention → future ARCs inherit the improvement`

A recurring defect discovered in an ARC must not remain a local patch if it reveals a general Factory weakness. Where reusable, the lesson should be promoted into the Factory template, validator, test, policy, recovery mechanism or production gate.

Conversely, a Factory rule that looks correct on paper but repeatedly fails under live operation is insufficient and must be refined using OMEGA evidence.

## Production gate rule

No Factory-produced ARC may be described as satisfying Reliable Excellence solely because:

- generation completed;
- deployment succeeded;
- a gateway started;
- one health check passed;
- one user interaction succeeded;
- an aura/maturity label exists;
- a commercial tier exists;
- a prior historical gate once passed.

Missing material evidence means **UNPROVEN**.

## Escalation rule

OMEGA escalates to PRIME when:

- claimed maturity exceeds reliability evidence;
- material performance degrades;
- recovery fails or becomes unproven;
- recurring incidents reveal a systemic weakness;
- security/privacy/integrity boundaries regress;
- Factory output repeatedly requires the same manual repair;
- evidence is stale or contradictory;
- an ARC's quality posture materially diverges from its reported state.

PRIME directs remediation and determines whether a production/lifecycle stage may advance.

## Factory learning rule

FACTORY must treat OMEGA's verified recurring findings as production intelligence.

Every generally applicable finding should be evaluated for one of these upstream responses:

1. Factory template change;
2. schema change;
3. automated test;
4. policy-as-code rule;
5. birth gate;
6. runtime health check;
7. recovery/rollback mechanism;
8. evidence requirement;
9. documentation or operator safeguard;
10. prevention control.

The aim is to turn lessons into **system-enforced quality**.

## Reporting shorthand

OMEGA reports:

`What is actually reliable? What is degraded? What is unproven? What must PRIME know?`

FACTORY reports:

`What quality did we build in? What evidence must exist? What reusable defect prevention did we add?`

PRIME asks:

`Does current evidence justify advancement, and has the system learned from the defect?`

## Founder shorthand

> **FACTORY builds reliability in. OMEGA proves and protects it in operation. PRIME ensures the system acts on the truth.**
