## Canonical production shorthand

The preferred human-readable Factory sequence is:

`Repo → ARC → Telegram → Link → First Contact → V1`

This shorthand exists to make ARC manufacture operationally obvious without forcing the Founder/operator to reason through every internal F0–F6 stage during ordinary production.

It is an abstraction over the detailed Factory stages, not a replacement for their evidence gates.

## Step meanings

### Repo

PRIME opens the production cycle. OMEGA reserves/classifies identity and source boundary. FACTORY records genesis. Repository infrastructure creates or designates the one authoritative ARC home.

Target mature behavior: repository creation is performed inside the PRIME-orchestrated Factory cycle rather than manually by the Founder.

### ARC

Factory manufactures the ARC source package: identity, governance, Brain registry, runtime boundary/source, visual package, commercial entitlement, onboarding contract, evidence scaffolding, hermetic contract and PRIME/OMEGA/FACTORY pointers.

Creation/registration does not imply runtime activation or maturity.

### Telegram

PRIME coordinates the isolated ARC runtime and the ARC's customer-facing Telegram bot/channel, including model-capacity assignment, protected secrets/state/log boundaries, health/recovery controls and fail-closed unknown identity behavior.

Telegram is a customer-facing control/interaction surface. PRIME remains backend orchestration infrastructure.

### Link

Once the ARC's runtime/channel is technically ready and safe for Owner entry, the Owner receives the direct ARC link.

The Owner interacts with the ARC. The Owner should not need PRIME, GitHub, VPS access or Factory internals merely to use the ARC.

### First Contact

The Owner enters through the approved ARC channel. PRIME coordinates exact-candidate verification and explicit binding behind the scenes. The ARC performs its configured Owner-facing onboarding.

First contact begins the final proof cycle. It does **not** itself equal V1.

### V1

After binding, the ARC must demonstrate real useful work plus required persistence, recovery, privacy, isolation, identity and continuity evidence.

Only after that gate closes may evidence-derived maturity be recorded as:

`V1 Foundation → Blue aura`

Repo existence, source registration, runtime startup, a Telegram bot, a link, first greeting, Owner binding or visual form alone cannot grant V1.

## Backend responsibility shorthand

Behind the simple production line:

`PRIME orchestrates everything → OMEGA watches ARC truth → FACTORY records lifecycle → BRAIN STEWARD handles real Brain lifecycle/interconnections → ARC owns bounded domain execution → Telegram is the Owner-facing surface`

PRIME remains the active supervisor, director and orchestrator throughout the cycle. Specialized functions perform bounded responsibilities and return state/evidence to PRIME.

## Mapping to detailed Factory stages

- `Repo` ≈ F0–F1: authorization, reservation, genesis and authoritative source boundary.
- `ARC` ≈ F2–F3: instance manufacture and stewardship registration.
- `Telegram` ≈ F4: runtime/channel/capacity provisioning and technical safety.
- `Link` = F4 exposure gate after technical readiness; not a maturity event.
- `First Contact` ≈ F5: explicit Owner binding, onboarding and first real work.
- `V1` ≈ F6 evidence closeout when V1 criteria are actually satisfied.

The mapping is intentionally approximate because a production stage may contain prerequisites serving more than one human-readable step.

## Mature Founder experience

Factory product quality should converge toward a Founder request such as:

`Prime, create an ARC for Narek.`

PRIME then orchestrates the full backend sequence until the ARC is ready for Owner entry. In the ideal standard case, the next ordinary Founder action is essentially to send the Owner the ready ARC link.

Human approval remains mandatory for protected decisions such as identity/Owner binding, external paid side effects, accepted visual form where required, verified domain authority and other explicit Founder/Owner approval gates.
