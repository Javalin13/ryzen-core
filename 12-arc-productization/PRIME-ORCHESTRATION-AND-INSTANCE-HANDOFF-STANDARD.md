# PRIME Orchestration & Instance Handoff Standard

Status: Founder-directed active prototype standard
Created: 2026-09-11

## Founder decision

**PRIME remains the active supervisor, director and orchestrator of the complete ARC production and lifecycle flow during the prototype era.**

PRIME does not surrender orchestration when OMEGA, FACTORY, BRAIN STEWARD or an ARC performs its specialized work. Those instances/functions operate under bounded responsibility and report state/evidence back into PRIME's orchestration loop.

Canonical ontology remains:

`Creator → RYZ3N → ARCs → Brains → Agents → Execution`

PRIME, OMEGA, FACTORY and BRAIN STEWARD are prototype control/stewardship infrastructure around that ontology; they are not new canonical tiers.

## Control principle

> **PRIME orchestrates the cycle. OMEGA stewards ARC population. FACTORY records lifecycle truth. BRAIN STEWARD governs Brain coherence. Each ARC owns its own bounded domain execution.**

No instance may silently absorb another instance's authority.

## Responsibility matrix

### PRIME — supervisor / director / orchestrator

PRIME owns the operational control loop. PRIME:

- receives Founder-authorized creation/lifecycle intent;
- opens and tracks the ARC production cycle;
- directs OMEGA to classify/reserve/register ARC-level facts;
- directs FACTORY lifecycle record creation/update through the approved stewardship path;
- directs repository provisioning through approved infrastructure capability;
- coordinates ARC package manufacture from the versioned Factory contract;
- coordinates BRAIN STEWARD only when Brain-level lifecycle facts exist;
- coordinates runtime provisioning, channel setup, health/isolation testing and recovery checks;
- blocks progression when any required instance reports contradiction/failure;
- keeps stage ordering coherent;
- receives status/evidence from all participating instances;
- reports material progress, blockers and decisions to Founder;
- preserves Founder approval gates and external-side-effect boundaries.

PRIME must not replace the ARC's detailed domain source, invent maturity, ingest unrestricted customer-private memory, or let orchestration become unlimited authority.

### OMEGA — ARC-population steward under PRIME

OMEGA performs delegated ARC-level stewardship. OMEGA:

- reserves/checks stable `arc_id`;
- classifies source-boundary type;
- checks source uniqueness and rejects dual-active truth;
- maintains ARC registry coherence;
- tracks ownership/lifecycle/form/maturity pointers;
- validates ARC-level registration completeness;
- detects ARC-level drift/contradiction;
- reports findings and blockers to PRIME;
- coordinates bounded ARC-level updates with FACTORY.

OMEGA does not independently direct the whole Factory cycle, provision runtime by itself, own customer domain reasoning, or overrule PRIME/Founder intent.

### FACTORY — lifecycle/provenance register under PRIME stewardship

FACTORY is the durable production/lifecycle record. FACTORY:

- records genesis before repository creation when applicable;
- records source creation/designation and provenance;
- records production-stage transitions;
- records runtime/binding/evidence status;
- records reset/transfer/migration/history;
- preserves immutable historical truth rather than silently rewriting provenance.

FACTORY does not reason, orchestrate, create Brains, grant permissions or execute customer work.

### BRAIN STEWARD — Brain-level coherence under PRIME

BRAIN STEWARD is invoked when an ARC contains/creates/changes Brains. It:

- validates Brain ownership by exactly one ARC;
- validates reasoning scope/non-scope;
- validates Agent authority/delegation boundaries;
- validates Brain lifecycle/interconnect/evidence;
- reports ARC-level implications to OMEGA and PRIME.

BRAIN STEWARD does not register ARC population, run FACTORY, or invent Brains without ARC/Owner need.

### ARC instance — bounded domain owner/executor

The ARC:

- owns its detailed source/configuration inside its authoritative repository;
- serves its Owner/domain;
- manages its own permitted memory/tasks/domain competence;
- creates/uses Brains according to canonical lifecycle rules;
- executes only within granted permissions/capacity;
- produces evidence/status upward without leaking unrestricted private payload.

The ARC does not rewrite RYZ3N canon, mutate other ARC repositories, self-promote maturity/aura, or commandeer PRIME/OMEGA/FACTORY authority.

## Orchestrated Factory flow

Every stage is orchestrated by PRIME:

`Founder intent`
`→ PRIME opens production cycle`
`→ PRIME directs OMEGA identity/source classification`
`→ PRIME confirms FACTORY genesis record`
`→ PRIME directs repository create/designate capability`
`→ PRIME directs Factory package manufacture`
`→ PRIME requests OMEGA registration validation`
`→ PRIME confirms FACTORY active/pre-runtime record`
`→ PRIME maintains supervision mirror`
`→ PRIME coordinates runtime/channel/capacity provisioning`
`→ PRIME coordinates explicit Owner binding`
`→ ARC performs real first-run/useful work`
`→ PRIME gathers evidence from ARC + OMEGA + BRAIN STEWARD as applicable`
`→ PRIME evaluates stage completion against canonical evidence gates`
`→ truthful lifecycle/maturity state recorded`

The phrase “OMEGA/FACTORY handles creation” must never be interpreted as PRIME stepping out of the loop. PRIME delegates specialized responsibilities while retaining orchestration.

## Handoff rule

Every handoff must specify:

- sending instance;
- receiving instance/function;
- exact requested action;
- authority boundary;
- expected artifact/result;
- blocking conditions;
- evidence/checkpoint pointer;
- return path to PRIME.

A handoff is incomplete until PRIME receives the expected result or an explicit blocker.

## No silent progression

PRIME must not advance a production stage merely because a file exists. Examples:

- repo exists ≠ source package complete;
- source package exists ≠ OMEGA registered;
- OMEGA registered ≠ runtime live;
- runtime live ≠ Owner bound;
- Owner bound ≠ V1 earned;
- profile image exists ≠ aura earned.

## Telegram/control surfaces

Founder Telegram or another authorized PRIME control surface can initiate or inspect the cycle. Telegram is a surface; PRIME is the orchestrator. OMEGA/FACTORY/BRAIN STEWARD remain backend specialized functions unless explicitly exposed for operational diagnostics.

## Future native RYZ3N

Native RYZ3N is expected to absorb these proven orchestration/stewardship contracts later. The current PRIME-centered implementation is prototype-era operational architecture, not a permanent new canonical tier.

## Founder invariant

> **PRIME supervises, directs and orchestrates the whole cycle. Specialized instances perform their bounded responsibilities and report back.**
