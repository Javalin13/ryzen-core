---

## Observation

What actually happened?

## User impact

What did the user experience, lose, gain or fail to complete?

## Context

What was the user trying to accomplish?

## Workaround / intervention

What did the Founder/operator/ARC do to solve or bypass it during the prototype?

## Reusable lesson

Generalize the lesson so it can apply to future ARCs without copying confidential customer content.

## Candidate UX/system change

What should be tested or changed?

## Evidence needed

What would confirm that this belongs in the final ARC UX/system?

## Validation target

Which later pilot, user type or scenario should validate the change?

## Commercial implication

Does this affect tier boundaries, support burden, onboarding, cost or willingness to pay?
