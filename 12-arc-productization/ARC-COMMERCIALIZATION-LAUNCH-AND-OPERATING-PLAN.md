## 1. Objective

Turn the proven ARC architecture into a repeatable paid product for entrepreneurs who want a useful personal/business AI operating partner without having to install, configure, maintain or understand the underlying technical stack.

The initial commercial target is not "everyone who uses AI". The first ideal customer is:

> **A solo entrepreneur or very small business owner who already understands the value of AI, but does not want to become an AI infrastructure engineer.**

They should not need to install Hermes, configure Telegram bots, manage model/provider accounts, maintain VPS infrastructure, handle API keys, diagnose agent failures or calculate model consumption.

The ARC offer absorbs that complexity into a managed recurring service.

## 2. Customer-facing positioning

Do **not** lead with the implementation stack.

Do not sell:

> "We install Hermes for you."

That is an infrastructure feature and is increasingly commoditized.

Primary positioning:

> **ARC — Your AI operating partner. It grows with you.**

Working plain-language value proposition:

> **Your own AI operating partner for about €50/month. It learns your business, remembers your work and helps you execute from the tools you already use — without servers, API keys or surprise technical complexity.**

Possible campaign line:

> **Your first AI employee. You talk. Your ARC works.**

The strongest ARC differentiation is the complete managed system around the model:

- isolated owner-specific ARC runtime;
- persistent identity/context and authorized memory;
- tasks, drafting, research, coding, files/websites and business support;
- progressive Brains, Agents and reusable capabilities;
- managed infrastructure and model-capacity stewardship;
- privacy/isolation boundaries;
- predictable subscription rather than requiring the customer to manage provider billing;
- verified V1→V6 maturity progression;
- maturity-derived aura/shine;
- privacy-safe capability reuse across the ARC network;
- eventual cross-device / JARVIS-style and gamified ecosystem evolution.

Technology such as Hermes, model providers, VPSs and Telegram is implementation detail unless a technical buyer specifically asks.

This market is **not empty**. That is useful validation, but it means ARC must compete on productization and experience rather than claiming to be the only hosted AI agent.

### Competitive conclusion

Do not make public claims such as:

- "nothing like this exists";
- "the only personal AI assistant";
- "the first managed Hermes service".

The defensible current claim is that ARC combines a managed owner-specific runtime, persistent context, privacy/isolation, operational stewardship, predictable subscription economics, verified maturity progression and privacy-safe reusable capabilities into one product system.

## 4. Launch strategy — Founding 20 first, advertising second

The goal of the first launch phase is **proof of willingness to pay + retention + manageable unit economics**, not maximum reach.

### Phase A — reference proof

Complete the real VONDA reference cycle and continue collecting real runtime/capacity/support evidence.

VONDA remains the tutorial/reference ARC. Do not turn VONDA itself into the mass-market customer template without first freezing reusable patterns and removing customer-specific payload.

### Phase B — Founding 20

Recruit the first **10–20 paying entrepreneurs** primarily through:

- personal network;
- face-to-face conversations;
- introductions/referrals;
- WhatsApp/DM outreach where appropriate;
- LinkedIn/network contacts;
- existing business relationships.

This is intentionally high-touch because the objective is to discover what users actually value, where onboarding breaks, what they repeatedly ask for and how much human support each ARC consumes.

### Founding 20 offer

Working launch offer:

> **Founding ARC — €49/month, founding rate locked while continuously subscribed.**

This is a launch-cohort offer, not a replacement of the current canonical Standard pricing model.

After the Founding 20, normal Standard pricing remains:

- **€50/month**, or
- **€500/year**.

Founding-20 setup should be free unless the requested work is clearly outside Standard scope.

The Founder may close the Founding 20 early if operational support, provisioning capacity or model economics show that further onboarding would reduce service quality.

### Phase C — proof assets

Before scaling paid media, collect permissioned proof such as:

- short customer quote/testimonial;
- one concrete before/after workflow;
- time saved or work completed;
- screen recording showing a real ARC request → execution → result;
- customer retention/continued use;
- anonymized operational metrics where useful.

Never expose private ARC conversations, memory or business data in marketing without explicit permission.

### Phase D — organic content

Priority channels:

- TikTok / Reels / Shorts for visual demonstrations;
- LinkedIn for founder/business use cases;
- referrals and customer introductions;
- direct demos.

Preferred creative format:

> **Show the ARC doing work. Do not rely on an AI avatar merely talking about AI.**

An AI narrator or avatar can be tested, but the proof should be the product interaction and finished outcome.

### Phase E — controlled paid advertising

Do not materially scale Meta/TikTok ads until the early cohort shows that customers activate, use and retain the product at healthy economics.

Paid campaigns start as small experiments, not as a mass launch.

Test separately:

- founder pain / "too many hats";
- no technical setup;
- one personal AI operating partner;
- predictable monthly cost;
- concrete workflow demos;
- ARC growth/maturity story.

Scale only advertisements that create retained paying customers, not merely clicks or leads.

## 5. Usage & billing promise

ARC should not expose ordinary customers to model-provider billing complexity.

The external promise for Standard should be:

> **One predictable subscription with standard AI usage included. No API keys to manage and no surprise pass-through model bill.**

Do **not** advertise "unlimited AI".

Do **not** promise that every possible workload will always remain inside €50/month.

### Standard fair-use principle

Standard includes normal entrepreneur/personal-business usage on shared infrastructure under monitored fair use.

Standard does not include by default:

- unlimited autonomous high-frequency jobs;
- large-scale data processing;
- continuous bulk generation;
- unlimited custom integrations;
- dedicated model capacity;
- heavy coding/build pipelines running continuously;
- unlimited human support;
- business-critical 24/7 SLA.

If usage materially exceeds Standard economics or creates capacity risk, the customer is contacted before commercial treatment changes. The normal remedies are:

1. optimize the workflow;
2. move the ARC to another capacity pool;
3. upgrade to Pro/Dedicated;
4. agree a clearly priced additional usage/capacity arrangement.

Never silently forward an unexpected provider overage to a Standard customer.

## 6. Capacity and service-quality rules

The existing Founder capacity doctrine remains binding:

> **Maximum 10 Standard ARCs per Ollama/Hermes model-capacity pool until production telemetry proves another ceiling preserves service quality.**

Split capacity earlier whenever telemetry shows recurring:

- throttling / rate limits;
- retry bursts;
- latency degradation;
- concurrency pressure;
- quota/headroom risk;
- model/provider incidents;
- one ARC consuming disproportionate capacity.

Every commercial ARC must have an attributable `arc_id`, model-capacity-pool assignment and privacy-safe consumption telemetry inherited from the Golden ARC Blueprint.

### Commercial heavy-usage trigger

A Standard ARC should be reviewed for optimization or tier migration when one or more are structurally true:

- it repeatedly triggers disproportionate-consumption telemetry;
- direct model/provider cost becomes commercially unhealthy relative to subscription revenue;
- it requires dedicated capacity to preserve other customers' service quality;
- support/customization becomes materially higher than Standard assumptions;
- it requires business-critical integrations or availability expectations outside Standard scope.

Exact numerical fair-use thresholds should **not** be published until enough production data from VONDA + Founding 20 exists to set them credibly.

## 7. Product maturity is separate from billing tier

Commercial plan and maturity version are related but not identical.

- **Billing tier** describes commercial scope, resource allowance, integrations, support and infrastructure.
- **ARC maturity V1→V6** describes verified capability maturity.

A customer cannot simply buy a false Gold/Platinum aura.

Aura remains evidence-derived:

`V1 Blue → V2 Cyan → V3 Violet → V4 Gold → V5 Platinum → V6 Sovereign/Prismatic`

Higher commercial tiers may provide the resources/integrations that allow an ARC to mature faster, but maturity must still be earned and verified.

## 8. Current operating team / responsibility map

This section intentionally distinguishes human decision authority from technical agents/runtime roles.

### Founder

Owns:

- product vision and final commercial decisions;
- positioning and customer promise;
- price changes and exceptions;
- first-wave sales and relationship building;
- Founder/strategic approvals;
- maturity doctrine and governance;
- deciding when paid media is allowed to scale;
- final acceptance of major product direction.

### PRIME — execution / technical steward

PRIME is not the commercial product and is not above the canonical RYZ3N hierarchy.

Current responsibilities:

- instantiate/provision ARCs from the Golden ARC Blueprint;
- runtime health and recovery;
- isolation verification;
- model-capacity telemetry and pool stewardship;
- implementation of approved reusable patterns;
- evidence-backed GREEN/RED reporting;
- preserve privacy-safe reusable lessons;
- flag capacity/support/technical risks before they become customer problems.

PRIME may not change prices, invent commercial promises, lower privacy/isolation standards or redefine maturity/aura without Founder direction.

### Luxcalibur — independent strategy/review layer

Current responsibilities:

- commercial/market benchmarking;
- second-line architecture/product review;
- cross-check PRIME reports/evidence through the GitHub bridge;
- translate Founder decisions into durable RYZ3N Core doctrine/directives;
- detect drift between business promise, architecture and evidence;
- help design pricing, positioning, launch experiments and scale gates.

Lux is not a customer-facing ARC and is not a canonical parent in the final RYZ3N hierarchy.

### Customer ARC

The ARC serves the Owner/customer inside its authorized scope. It must not expose PRIME topology, other ARC state or another customer's private payload.

### Future human functions — not yet assigned to named people

As scale requires it, separate functions should emerge for:

- onboarding/customer success;
- support/operations;
- sales/growth;
- finance/billing/admin;
- legal/privacy/compliance;
- infrastructure/security engineering.

Do not assign these functions to a named person unless the Founder explicitly confirms that responsibility.

## 9. Team growth triggers

### 0–20 paid ARCs

- Founder leads sales + customer discovery.
- Founder participates in onboarding enough to understand user behavior.
- PRIME provisions/stewards runtime and capacity.
- Lux reviews productization, market and bridge evidence.
- Optimize repeatability before hiring around avoidable manual work.

### ~20–50 paid ARCs

Add or assign customer-success/onboarding capacity when Founder/support workload starts interfering with sales/product direction or when support becomes a material recurring weekly burden.

Provisioning should increasingly become automated from the Golden ARC Blueprint.

### ~50–100 paid ARCs

Separate customer support/operations from Founder sales/product work. Formalize billing, incident handling, onboarding scripts, service boundaries and capacity planning.

### 100+ paid ARCs

Operate ARC as a real subscription platform with dedicated operational ownership, measurable SLAs for higher tiers, stronger security/compliance processes, structured growth experimentation and capacity planning by pool/segment.

These population bands are operating guidance, not hard organizational law. Workload evidence overrides headcount vanity.

## 10. Metrics that must be known before scaling

Track at minimum:

### Commercial

- paid ARC count;
- MRR / ARR;
- monthly vs annual mix;
- lead → paid conversion;
- referral share;
- cancellation/churn;
- 30/60/90-day retention;
- CAC once paid acquisition begins;
- payback period.

### Product

- onboarding completion rate;
- time to first useful outcome;
- weekly active owners;
- recurring workflows per ARC;
- number of useful Brains/capabilities adopted;
- customer-reported value/time saved;
- progression/maturity evidence where relevant.

### Operations

- provisioning time per ARC;
- human support minutes per ARC/month;
- incident rate;
- p50/p95 model latency;
- retries/throttles/errors;
- model consumption per ARC;
- direct model/provider cost per ARC;
- capacity-pool utilization;
- heavy/disproportionate ARC flags;
- VPS/process/resource footprint;
- recovery/intervention burden.

### Margin

Know both:

1. incremental gross margin per ARC; and
2. fully loaded contribution after support/admin/shared-platform allocation.

A €50 ARC that requires large amounts of custom human work is not a healthy Standard customer even if its token cost is low.

## 11. Gate before materially scaling paid ads

Paid media may be tested early with small learning budgets, but broad spend should wait until there is evidence that the machine works.

Working scale gate:

- at least ~10 genuinely paying non-Founder users, preferably approaching the Founding 20;
- onboarding is repeatable;
- users reach a useful first outcome quickly;
- early retention is credible;
- no unresolved privacy/isolation risk;
- model-capacity telemetry is healthy;
- support load does not destroy margin;
- provisioning no longer requires bespoke architectural work for each user;
- at least 3 strong repeatable use cases are visible;
- at least some permissioned proof/testimonials exist.

If those conditions are weak, fix product/onboarding before buying more traffic.

## 12. Advertising claim discipline

Good claims:

- managed for you;
- no server setup required;
- no personal API-key management required;
- standard AI usage included under the plan's fair-use policy;
- one persistent ARC that can learn authorized context and workflows;
- isolated customer ARC architecture;
- grows in verified capabilities over time;
- fixed subscription price for the included Standard scope.

Avoid unsupported/scare claims such as:

- "doing this yourself will cost hundreds of thousands";
- "API calls always become extremely expensive";
- "ARC is the only solution on the market";
- "unlimited AI for €50";
- "guaranteed autonomous employee replacement";
- unverified percentage/time-saving claims.

The strongest marketing is observable product proof, not fear of technical catastrophe.

## 13. Initial revenue landmarks

At a €49 Founding rate:

- 20 Founding ARCs = **€980 MRR**.

At €50 Standard monthly pricing:

- 100 monthly ARCs = **€5,000 MRR** before annual-plan mix, higher tiers and implementation revenue;
- 1,000 monthly ARCs = **€50,000 MRR** before churn, tax, discounts, costs and tier mix.

These are arithmetic revenue illustrations, not forecasts.
