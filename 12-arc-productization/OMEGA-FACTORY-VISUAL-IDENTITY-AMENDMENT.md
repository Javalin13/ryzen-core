## Founder decision

Visual identity is durable ARC product state.

The existing OMEGA/FACTORY birth and lifecycle contract is therefore amended additively so that every owner-facing autonomous ARC has a discoverable ARC-owned visual identity package and PRIME can resolve it without copying the full asset/history into PRIME.

This amendment does not create a new canonical tier and does not change ARC maturity.

## Authority split

### RYZ3N Core
Owns the universal ARC visual-family grammar and machine-readable manifest schema.

### Owning ARC repository
Owns the detailed visual truth:

- `arc/visual/ARC-VISUAL-IDENTITY.md` or equivalent;
- `arc/visual/visual-identity.json` or equivalent;
- canonical/current portrait asset;
- ARC-specific archetype and presentation;
- domain motifs and form rules;
- distilled Founder/Owner feedback and rejected directions that materially shape future generations;
- form-version history.

The ARC repository is authoritative for its own face/form.

### OMEGA
Owns coherence of safe ARC-level visual pointers and form state:

- visual record pointer;
- manifest pointer;
- canonical/current asset pointer or explicit pending state;
- archetype/presentation;
- current form version;
- Founder/Owner approval state;
- form-vs-aura consistency;
- material visual drift.

OMEGA does not invent or redesign the ARC and does not infer maturity from artwork.

### FACTORY
Preserves lifecycle/provenance facts:

- initial visual-package creation;
- approved form version;
- material supersession/reset events;
- current asset pointer;
- approval state;
- historical form-version transitions.

FACTORY does not store full binary assets or raw image-generation transcripts.

### PRIME
Uses OMEGA/FACTORY pointers to locate the owning ARC's current visual truth when:

- provisioning/restoring a Telegram/profile image;
- presenting an ARC in an owner-facing interface;
- generating a successor form;
- auditing visual drift;
- recovering continuity in a new session.

PRIME must fetch the ARC-owned record rather than regenerate the ARC from memory when a canonical asset already exists.

## Mandatory birth addition

For future owner-facing ARCs, the birth sequence becomes:

`authorized creation → reserve arc_id → prepare ARC-side source → initialize ARC-owned visual package → define archetype/domain form → Founder/Owner approval → register safe visual pointers with OMEGA/FACTORY → PRIME mirror → isolation/governance/runtime provisioning → evidence → truthful lifecycle state`

The portrait does not need to block backend provisioning when it is not operationally required, but an owner-facing/channel-facing ARC is not visually productized until its approved form is durably recorded.

## Visual-family default

Unless explicitly excepted by Founder/Owner decision, new ARC portraits inherit the RYZ3N family:

> **sleek premium humanoid synthetic intelligence with a human-readable face, coherent engineered body language, controlled domain symbolism and ARC-specific character.**

Do not default to mascot robots, cute rounded helper bots, chibi forms, screen-face emoji bots or unrelated one-off styles.

Uniformity means shared family DNA, not identical faces.

## Durable feedback rule

Material visual feedback must be reduced into reusable design decisions in the owning ARC repository.

Examples:

- mascot rejected in favor of humanoid;
- gender/presentation corrected;
- specific face/body treatment approved;
- domain environment approved;
- style judged too playful/robotic/generic;
- crop/profile constraints;
- family-alignment lessons.

Do not dump full private chats. Preserve decisions and reusable lessons.

## Form / aura boundary

The ARC portrait is **form**. Official maturity aura remains evidence-derived.

> **Form is Owner-resettable. Aura is maturity-derived.**

Domain colors or blue lighting in a V1-target portrait do not themselves prove V1 Blue is earned. OMEGA must keep form state and maturity/aura state separate.

## Current implementation examples

- VONDA: durable visual identity package exists; exact previously approved binary remains explicitly pending recovery rather than being guessed.
- Cargo ARC: Founder-approved `cargo-form-v1.0`, male humanoid synthetic-intelligence archetype, ARC-owned canonical portrait committed under `Javalin13/CargoConnect/arc/visual/assets/`, with OMEGA/FACTORY/PRIME pointers.
- NARC reservation: when creation begins, its own repository must initialize a separate visual package from the same family standard rather than cloning Cargo/VONDA.

## Founder shorthand

> **Each ARC owns its face. RYZ3N defines the family. PRIME knows where to find the face. OMEGA keeps the pointers coherent. FACTORY remembers how the form evolved. Aura still tells only the truth about maturity.**
