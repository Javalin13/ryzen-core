## Proof
Live Hermes source inspection established that `build_system_prompt_parts()` returns the canonical three tiers directly:

- `stable`
- `context`
- `volatile`

and that `build_system_prompt()` joins those tiers before provider/cache-capability routing decisions.

Observed source boundary:

- `agent/system_prompt.py:903-906` returns the three tier strings.
- `agent/system_prompt.py:927-929` joins the tiers into the full system prompt and assigns `_cached_system_prompt_static = parts["stable"]`.

## Architectural consequence
The correct provider-independent measurement seam is the prompt builder itself, not downstream cache-capability state such as `_cached_system_prompt_static` after provider gating and not `prompt_cache_boundary`.

The next instrumentation must therefore retain content-free counts at build time for:

- stable chars
- context chars
- volatile chars
- assembled total chars

These metrics must be available regardless of provider/model (Laguna, local Qwen, Super, Ultra, Anthropic, or future routes), must not persist raw prompt content, and must not alter prompt bytes or routing behavior.
