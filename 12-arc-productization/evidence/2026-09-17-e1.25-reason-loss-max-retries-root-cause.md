# E1.25 Reason-Loss at Max-Retries Fallback — 2026-09-17

Status: **ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED — behavior change not yet deployed**

## Evidence chain

A direct classifier probe against the live Laguna failure signature returned:

- `reason = FailoverReason.rate_limit`
- `retryable = True`

The main eager fallback branch in `agent/conversation_loop.py` preserves the classified reason correctly:

`agent._try_activate_fallback(reason=classified.reason)`

However, live PRIME logs around the same `ResourceExhausted` event showed no `Rate-limit backoff level ...` message, even though `try_activate_fallback()` arms `_rate_limited_until` when called with a `rate_limit` reason.

The later max-retries fallback block at approximately line 6372 instead calls:

`agent._try_activate_fallback()`

with **no classified reason**.

For a retryable `ResourceExhausted` path, this is the matching structural explanation for the observed behavior:

1. primary Laguna returns `ResourceExhausted`;
2. classifier returns `FailoverReason.rate_limit`;
3. eager fallback may be bypassed while the credential pool is considered recoverable;
4. max retries are reached;
5. the generic max-retries fallback activates Qwen without passing `classified.reason`;
6. `try_activate_fallback()` therefore receives `reason=None`;
7. the 60-second rate-limit cooldown is not armed;
8. the next turn restores/re-probes Laguna immediately instead of staying on the healthy fallback during cooldown.

## Why the ~6158 callsite is not the live path

The non-retryable client-error fallback around line 6158 also omits the reason, but the observed `ResourceExhausted` probe is retryable, so `is_client_error` does not match that path. The max-retries block is therefore the relevant live path for the current incident.

## Smallest correction

Preserve the existing classified reason at the max-retries fallback callsite:

`agent._try_activate_fallback(reason=classified.reason)`

No new circuit subsystem is required. Hermes already has the correct cross-turn cooldown and restore-primary gate; the health reason is simply lost at this later fallback seam.

## Acceptance test

After the one-line correction:

1. trigger or observe a Laguna `ResourceExhausted` event;
2. confirm log contains `Rate-limit backoff level 0: cooldown 60 s`;
3. confirm the current turn falls back once to Qwen and completes;
4. issue another turn within 60 seconds;
5. confirm Laguna is not re-probed and fallback remains active;
6. after cooldown, confirm automatic primary re-probe/recovery remains possible.

## Architectural implication

This is a replaceable-runtime adapter defect, not a RYZ3N cognition defect. The RYZ3N provider-health contract remains:

`classify health failure → preserve reason → arm bounded cooldown → degrade once → stay on healthy fallback during cooldown → probe primary after cooldown → restore automatically when healthy`.
