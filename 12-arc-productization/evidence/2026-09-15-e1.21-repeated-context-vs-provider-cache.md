## Interpretation

Approximately 98% of PRIME's live request is invariant stable prefix, yet NVIDIA reported zero cached input tokens. The current free endpoint therefore recomputes effectively the entire PRIME context on this request despite almost all request text being reusable across turns.

This strongly validates the cache/context-efficiency direction and explains why provider-side saturation is disproportionately costly for PRIME-sized workloads.

The same turn also proved that Laguna can be fast when admitted: ~4.75 seconds end-to-end for the provider call. Output exactness remained imperfect (`/think*` leakage/duplication), which is a separate behavior-quality issue and must not be conflated with cache efficiency.

## Decision

Do not change providers or redesign the architecture. The next necessary step is to determine whether the NVIDIA free endpoint/API can explicitly reuse the stable prefix. If explicit provider-side prefix caching is unavailable, RYZ3N must reduce/compile the stable prefix before transmission while preserving PRIME's constitutional/governance semantics.
