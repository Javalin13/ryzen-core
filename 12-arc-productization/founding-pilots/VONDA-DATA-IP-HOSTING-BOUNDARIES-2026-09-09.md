## Purpose

Clarify the commercial and technical boundary for the VONDA Corporation Founding Pilot without exposing RYZ3N's proprietary implementation stack.

## Hosting / domain principle

- The VONDA Corporation website and domain may remain technically managed through the hosting environment used by RYZ3N.
- If VONDA Corporation wants its own separate hosting contract/account, that external hosting cost is borne by VONDA Corporation.
- Current Founder-reported reference cost for that separate ALL-INKL hosting option is approximately **€25/month**.
- Where registrar/TLD rules allow it, the domain holder may be VONDA Corporation even when technical hosting/administration remains under the agreed RYZ3N environment.
- If the pilot ends and VONDA Corporation wants to move the domain/site to another provider, RYZ3N should reasonably cooperate with the applicable transfer/export procedure, subject to registrar/provider rules and external costs.

## Client ownership

VONDA Corporation retains ownership/control of:

- its brand;
- client-supplied content;
- VONDA-specific website content;
- business data;
- prospect/client data, subject to applicable law and service-operation requirements.

## RYZ3N proprietary IP

RYZ3N retains ownership and confidentiality over its proprietary implementation, including where applicable:

- internal AI/tool stack;
- model choices and routing;
- prompts and system instructions;
- orchestration logic;
- reusable automations;
- reusable templates/components;
- ARC architecture;
- internal methods, workflows and know-how.

The client does **not** receive a right to a complete disclosure of every AI tool, model, prompt, chain, routing rule or implementation detail used by RYZ3N.

## Required transparency boundary

This confidentiality does not override legal or contractual disclosure obligations.

If a third-party provider actually receives or processes VONDA Corporation personal/professional data, RYZ3N should disclose the relevant processor/subprocessor information to the extent required for lawful operation, data-processing terms or security transparency.

The principle is:

`disclose what touches client data when required; protect the proprietary stack that does not need to be disclosed`.

## Data retention / ARC learning

The pilot should not promise that every piece of data disappears immediately when the pilot ends.

RYZ3N may retain, subject to applicable law and the agreed retention rules:

- technical logs;
- security records;
- configuration history;
- system-performance metrics;
- user preferences necessary for continuity;
- generalized product learnings;
- anonymized or de-identified patterns used to improve ARC behavior and future product design;
- residual backups until their normal retention cycle expires.

However, identifiable prospect/client data or confidential VONDA-specific content should not be kept indefinitely merely to improve the product where there is no valid legal/contractual basis.

Preferred learning rule:

`client usage → extract reusable pattern → generalize / anonymize → improve ARC`

rather than:

`keep raw client/prospect data indefinitely`.

## Exit / portability principle

If the pilot stops:

1. VONDA Corporation may request export/restitution of reasonably exportable VONDA-owned content and data.
2. VONDA-specific directly identifiable data can be deleted in accordance with the agreed retention rules and applicable obligations.
3. Residual backups/logs may remain until their normal expiry cycle.
4. Generalized/anonymized product learnings may remain with RYZ3N.
5. RYZ3N's ARC platform, internal AI stack, prompts, orchestration, reusable code and proprietary know-how do not transfer.

## Commercial safeguard

No wording in the VONDA proposal should imply that:

- VONDA receives the complete RYZ3N technology stack;
- RYZ3N must disclose all AI tools;
- all technical history must be erased immediately regardless of operational/security/legal needs;
- the free pilot includes a separate €25/month hosting contract paid by RYZ3N if VONDA elects to have its own hosting account.

## Core rule

> **VONDA owns its business assets and data. RYZ3N protects its platform and know-how. Data-processing transparency applies where client data actually flows, not to every proprietary implementation detail.**
