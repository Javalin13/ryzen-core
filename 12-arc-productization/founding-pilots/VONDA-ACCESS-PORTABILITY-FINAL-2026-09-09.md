# VONDA Corporation — Final access, hosting and portability boundary

Date: 2026-09-09  
Status: Founder-approved final proposal boundary  
Applies to: VONDA Corporation × RYZ3N Founding Pilot

## Shared infrastructure access

The VONDA website and ARC may run inside infrastructure used by RYZ3N for multiple projects.

Therefore:

- master hosting credentials are not shared;
- administrator passwords for shared infrastructure are not shared;
- API keys, secrets, Supabase/backend master access and other shared-project credentials are not shared;
- VONDA Corporation must never receive access that could expose unrelated RYZ3N environments, data or other client/project resources.

## Scoped client access

Where the infrastructure/provider supports separate or restricted client access, VONDA Corporation may receive only the functional/scoped access reasonably necessary for its own website, content and designated functions.

Where the provider does **not** support a safely isolated client access model, the relevant master/administrator access remains exclusively under RYZ3N control. RYZ3N then performs the required technical administration for the client environment.

The absence of direct administrator access to shared infrastructure does not remove VONDA Corporation's rights over its own brand, content and business data.

## Hosting and future provider choice

The domain and website hosting remain technically managed in the hosting environment used by RYZ3N unless the parties later decide otherwise.

If VONDA Corporation later chooses its own hosting account or another hosting provider, the external costs associated with that choice are for VONDA Corporation.

RYZ3N's supplier selection, commercial sourcing rationale and internal vendor economics are provider know-how and do not need to be disclosed to the client.

## Domain transfer / holder change / migration

Any requested change of domain holder, transfer of domain, naming arrangement or migration to another hosting provider remains subject to the technical and administrative possibilities of:

- the registrar;
- the relevant TLD/domain extension;
- the current provider;
- the destination provider;
- applicable registration rules.

RYZ3N will cooperate reasonably with an eligible transfer or migration but does **not** guarantee that every requested holder change, transfer, naming arrangement or migration is technically or administratively possible.

## Portability

If the pilot ends, VONDA Corporation may request restitution/export of reasonably exportable client-owned content and data, where technically possible.

Portability of client-owned content/data does not imply transfer of:

- the shared ARC platform;
- reusable RYZ3N components;
- shared hosting/backend environments;
- prompts or prompt systems;
- routing/orchestration logic;
- internal AI tooling/model choices;
- API keys/secrets/master credentials;
- proprietary methods, configurations or reusable know-how.

## Data retention and ARC learning

VONDA Corporation business information is not used by RYZ3N for unrelated commercial purposes.

Certain technical/operational information may remain where necessary for ARC operation, continuity, personalization, security, backups and product improvement. This can include usage history, preferences, configuration state, technical metadata/logs, security records, residual backups and generalized product learnings.

Where possible and appropriate, product learnings should be generalized or separated from directly identifying prospect/client data and confidential VONDA-specific content.

## Core rule

**Client ownership of content/data does not equal root access to shared infrastructure.**

**Portability does not equal guaranteed transfer feasibility.**

**Client transparency does not require disclosure of RYZ3N's proprietary technology stack or supplier strategy.**
