# VONDA Corporation — Domain / Hosting Decision

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: active implementation note

## Decision

The VONDA Corporation domain may remain technically hosted and administered through the RYZ3N hosting environment during the Founding Pilot.

The domain registration holder is treated separately from the hosting account. ALL-INKL provides a Domain-Inhaberwechsel / Ownerchange procedure, so where the applicable registrar and top-level-domain rules permit it, the official domain holder can be changed to VONDA Corporation without requiring the website hosting itself to leave the RYZ3N hosting environment.

If VONDA Corporation later wants to move technical domain management to its own hosting account or another provider, RYZ3N will cooperate with the applicable transfer process (for example an authorization code/provider transfer or an internal ALL-INKL transfer), subject to registrar/TLD rules and any external fees.

## Contract wording principle

- Hosting/platform administration may remain under RYZ3N during the pilot.
- VONDA Corporation retains its brand, content, business data and client-specific assets.
- Where supported, VONDA Corporation may be the official domain registrant/holder even while RYZ3N provides hosting and technical administration.
- Laetitia receives the site/admin access reasonably required for the pilot.
- On exit, VONDA-specific content and reasonably exportable data remain portable.
- If requested, RYZ3N cooperates with a later domain transfer to VONDA Corporation's own account/provider.
- RYZ3N generic ARC/platform components and reusable IP do not transfer with the client domain/content.

## Provider evidence checked

ALL-INKL publicly provides:
- a Domain-Inhaberwechsel (Ownerchange) form;
- an internal transfer mechanism to another ALL-INKL contract;
- provider-transfer procedures using the method applicable to the relevant TLD.

This note supersedes any earlier wording implying that domain ownership necessarily requires the domain to leave the RYZ3N hosting platform.
