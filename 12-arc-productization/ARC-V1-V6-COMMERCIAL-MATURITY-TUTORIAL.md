## 1. The one-minute commercial story

A customer does not buy a finished monolithic AI. They start with a useful ARC V1 and grow it as their needs and operating maturity increase.

> **Start useful. Become operational. Become specialized. Reach autonomous recursive cognition. Add natural JARVIS-style presence. Enter the gamified RYZ3N ecosystem.**

The ARC is never trained or built from zero at each step. It inherits the proven ARC platform and can adopt validated reusable modules, Brain contracts, Agent patterns and workflow components already learned elsewhere in the ARC network, while client-private data, memory, secrets and permissions remain isolated.

Commercial shorthand:

`V1 Foundation → V2 Operations → V3 Specialist → V4 Golden Recursive ARC → V5 Platinum Presence → V6 Sovereign Gamified Ecosystem`

## 3. The V4 threshold — what “complete ARC” means

V4 is the first point at which the ARC should be considered substantially complete as an **Autonomous Recursive Cognition System (ARCS)** node.

This is not a claim of general AGI. The target is practical completeness of the RYZ3N ARC architecture:

`Owner Intent → Domain/Project → Intent → Activity → Brain → Agents/Tools → Execution → Evidence → Memory/Continuation → Reflection/Improvement → next action`

A V4 ARC can coherently:

- understand the Owner's current intent and context;
- choose or create an appropriate specialist Brain when justified;
- delegate to Agents/tools under permissions;
- execute multi-step work;
- verify outcomes and preserve evidence;
- retain validated know-how and continuation state;
- coordinate multiple Brains without collapsing their scopes;
- reuse validated modules from the wider ARC ecosystem;
- escalate material risk/governance issues to the steward layer;
- improve its operating patterns from evidence without silently rewriting its mission or permissions.

V4 therefore represents **autonomous recursive cognition completeness inside bounded governance**, not unrestricted AGI.

## 4. Every ARC is born from the network, not from scratch

Commercially and architecturally, a new ARC should be presented as an instance of a growing intelligence ecosystem.

The customer receives:

1. the proven **Golden ARC Blueprint** / shared runtime foundation;
2. the ARC's own isolated identity, memory, user permissions, secrets and business/personal context;
3. access to a registry of **validated reusable capability modules**;
4. the ability to add specialist Brains and Agents as the user's real needs emerge;
5. improvements from generalized, non-confidential patterns proven by other ARCs;
6. future participation in stewarded cross-ARC intelligence convergence where authorized.

This means Fleet ARC can contribute a reusable dispatch/operations reasoning pattern, Cargo ARC can contribute freight/procurement patterns, VONDA can contribute business/coaching/operational patterns, and future ARCs can contribute their own validated specializations. Another ARC may reuse those **contracts/modules/patterns** without inheriting the originating user's private memory or client data.

Commercial message:

> **Your ARC does not begin alone. It joins an evolving network of proven capabilities while remaining privately yours.**

## 5. Cross-ARC capability federation rule

“Connected to all ARCs” must never mean unrestricted cross-client memory access.

The reusable federation layer may share or expose, according to authorization and maturity:

- validated Brain templates and reasoning contracts;
- Agent/tool contracts;
- workflow modules;
- memory schemas;
- verification criteria;
- generalized operational lessons;
- capability metadata and specialization registry;
- explicit scoped cross-ARC services where both sides authorize them.

It must not automatically share:

- private conversations;
- personal/client memory;
- client documents or contact lists;
- secrets/credentials;
- confidential commercial payloads;
- unrestricted permissions.

The architecture principle remains:

> **Share capability, not private payload. Reuse intelligence patterns, not another owner's memory.**

## 6. Commercial progression principle

The maturity ladder is a product journey, not merely software versioning.

A customer should always know:

- what version their ARC is currently at;
- what it can reliably do now;
- what the next maturity target unlocks;
- what evidence is required to level up;
- which modules are already available from the network;
- which capabilities require new integrations, usage capacity or higher commercial scope.

This creates an understandable upgrade path while preventing V1 from being oversold as V4.

## 7. Relationship to existing RYZ3N doctrine

This maturity tutorial sits above and does not replace the canonical hierarchy:

`Creator → RYZ3N → ARCs → Brains → Agents → Execution`

## 10. Form reset, aura invariance and ownership transfer

The ARC's **form/look** and its **aura** are separate concepts.

The Owner may reset the ARC's visual form, body, shell, skin or layout back to its factory presentation. That cosmetic reset does **not** change the ARC's maturity state and therefore does **not** change its aura.

Example:

`V4 ARC + factory-form reset → still V4 → still Gold aura`

The aura is not an owner-selectable color. It is a deterministic visual expression of verified maturity.

A different rule applies when an ARC is sold or transferred and the transfer includes an actual reset of owner-specific capacity, competence, evidence or state. If the ARC becomes effectively empty for the new Owner, it must return to the Foundation state:

`Transferred + emptied/reset ARC → V1 Foundation → Blue aura`

Transfer alone does not automatically force V1. The decisive question is whether the competence/evidence needed to truthfully support the previous maturity survives and remains valid after the transfer.
