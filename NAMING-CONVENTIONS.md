## Official spelling

**RYZ3N** is the official spelling for the project, brand, commercial references, client-facing material, agreements, proposals, websites and formal documentation.

## Informal shorthand

**Ryzen** may still appear as an informal spoken/written shorthand in conversation or legacy/internal notes, but it is not the preferred official spelling.

## Rule

When creating or revising formal documents, use:

`RYZ3N`

Do not introduce `RYZEN` as an alternate formal spelling.

Where older documents use `Ryzen` or `RYZEN` in a client-facing/commercial context, align them to **RYZ3N** when they are next materially revised.

## Architecture terminology

When referring to the ecosystem/architecture in formal documentation, prefer **RYZ3N architecture**, **RYZ3N ecosystem**, or the exact named component (ARC, ARCs, PRIME, etc.). Informal conversation may still use “Ryzen” as shorthand when context is clear.
