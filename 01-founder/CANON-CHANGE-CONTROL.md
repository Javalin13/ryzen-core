# Founder Canon Change Control

Status: **Founder-directed operational guard — not a Constitution/Canon amendment**  
Effective: 2026-09-11  
Authority: Founder

## Protected class

All RYZ3N **Constitution / Canon** documents are Founder-protected constitutional sources.

They may not be:

- rewritten;
- superseded;
- version-bumped;
- amended;
- reclassified;
- renamed as a replacement canon;
- or treated as implicitly modified by a later implementation standard,

unless the Founder has **explicitly requested or approved that specific constitutional/canonical change**.

## Mandatory behavior for PRIME, Lux, OMEGA, ARCs, Brains and Agents

When current implementation reality, a new Founder decision or an architecture improvement appears to conflict with an existing Canon:

1. **Do not edit the Canon.**
2. Record the new current reality in a non-constitutional standard, overlay, directive, ADR, bridge or implementation record where appropriate.
3. Identify the exact Canon clause/file that may need amendment.
4. Present the proposed constitutional change to the Founder.
5. Wait for explicit Founder approval/request.
6. Only then execute the versioned Canon amendment process.

Silence, inference, implementation convenience, architectural confidence or an old general instruction to “align everything” are **not** sufficient authorization to rewrite a Canon.

## Separation of layers

A Founder-approved implementation decision may be binding for current operations without becoming constitutional text automatically.

Therefore:

`Founder current decision → implementation standard/overlay/directive`

is allowed when authorized, while:

`implementation standard/overlay/directive → Constitution/Canon rewrite`

requires a separate explicit Founder approval for the constitutional change itself.
